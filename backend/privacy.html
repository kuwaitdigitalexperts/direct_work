Direct Work (دايركت وورك)

State of Kuwait (دولة الكويت) • Privacy Policy • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Direct Work ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy in compliance with applicable laws in the State of Kuwait and Google Play Developer Policies. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and services collect, use, store, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Direct Work as a Job Seeker, Employer, or Service Provider, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Full name, phone number, email address, password, account type (Job Seeker or Company/Employer), and nationality.
  • Job Seeker Profiles & Applications: Profession, work experience, age, Kuwait governorate of residence, curriculum vitae (CV/Resume in PDF or document format), professional certificates, and profile photos.
  • Employer & Company Information: Company name, commercial registration/license number, contact person details, business address, and job vacancy specifications.
  • Communications: Messages exchanged between employers and applicants through in-app channels or direct contact numbers provided voluntarily.
  • Device & Usage Data: Basic diagnostic logs, app version, and device type for performance optimization and error monitoring.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate recruitment and service connectivity purposes:

  • To connect job seekers with licensed employers and business owners in the State of Kuwait.
  • To display job applicant cards and vacancy listings according to sectors and professions chosen by users.
  • To facilitate direct communication (phone calls, WhatsApp, in-app messaging) between verified parties.
  • To process optional premium posting packages via authorized payment gateways (e.g., MyFatoorah Kuwait).
  • To provide customer support and notify you of application updates.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

Your information is only shared under the following conditions:

  • With Prospective Employers: Job seeker profile details (name, profession, CV, experience) are visible to employers searching for qualified candidates.
  • With Service Providers: Trusted infrastructure partners (e.g., cloud hosting, payment gateways) who assist in running the service under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by Kuwaiti regulatory authorities or applicable law.

5. Data Security & Storage

We apply robust administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention (Google Play Compliance)

User Rights & Permanent Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You may request the deletion of your account and all associated records at any time:

Option 1 (In-App): Go to My Account → Delete Account Permanently. Confirming this action will immediately remove your profile, CVs, photos, and job applications from our active database.

Option 2 (Online / Email): You can submit a deletion request by sending an email with your registered phone number or email address to support@directwork.site with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process and permanently purge your data within 48 hours.

7. Permissions Requested by the App

  • CAMERA: Used only when you choose to take a photo of your certificate, profile, or company license.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / STORAGE: Used solely to allow you to select and upload your CV (PDF/DOC) or resume photo from your device.
  • INTERNET: Required to connect to our secure servers and retrieve real-time job listings and sector updates.

8. Children's Privacy

Direct Work is an employment and professional services application intended for individuals of legal working age (18 years and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
